# v0.12.0 — Track selection, editable merges, Sketchpad Pulse

- Shift-click track headers to select a range; Command-click to toggle tracks. Delete or
  Remove Tracks deletes the selection, with one-step Undo. Command-A follows the active
  selection area: tracks after clicking a header, clips after clicking the timeline.
- Merge all clips through the track menu, or selected clips through a clip's context menu.
  Compatible editable-note clips using one instrument/preset keep their note data, including
  trimmed notes, silent padding and loop repetitions. The merged WAV preserves existing audio.
  Audio-only, third-party AU takes and incompatible presets still bounce to audio; Undo restores originals.
- Sketchpad Pulse adds six original Surge XT patches, selectable in the keyboard panel:
  Deep Pulse Kick, Round Sub, Rolling Harmonic Bass, Resonant Pulse Pluck, Metal Tick, Air Shaker.
  They are reference-inspired approximations based on spectral analysis, not sampled audio
  or a verified listening match. Requires the installed Surge XT Audio Unit.
- The bank can also be installed in Surge's user-patch folder without overwriting user edits.

Verification: native smoke checks; selective merge and subsequent note edit; atomic deletion/Undo;
Surge preset-state restore and finite, non-clipping output on every key from MIDI 24 through 100.
