# v0.12.1 — Draggable project ending

- Drag the yellow END handle on the top ruler to shorten or extend playback/export.
- Clips and editable notes beyond the end remain intact and visible in a dimmed area.
- Right-click END → Fit End to Clips restores the boundary to the last clip.
- The end is saved with the project and supports Undo/Redo. New recordings, imported
  clips and AI generations can extend it; ordinary deletions do not reset it.
- Playback/export skip clips past the ending and schedule crossing clips only up to it.
- Includes v0.12.0 track multi-selection, editable merges and Sketchpad Pulse.
