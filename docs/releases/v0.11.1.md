# AI Music Sketchpad 0.11.1

Playback performance update; project format and audio sample rate are unchanged.

- Reuse one player and audio file per clip across all its loop repetitions.
- Stop scheduling muted/non-solo tracks; resume at the current musical position when unmuted.
- Bypass inactive effects and muted chains, and clear pre-mute effect tails.
- Apply only changed effect parameters; prepare automation points once per edit rather than sorting every tick.
- Keep playhead, keyboard highlights and input-meter updates local to their views instead of invalidating the whole window.
- Draw the ruler in one canvas and update the visual cursor at approximately 30 Hz; active automation retains its approximately 60 Hz update cadence.
- Apply the same clip-player reuse to export and skip muted tracks entirely when exporting.

Regression checks cover sample-accurate trimmed/overlapping loops, 12 tracks with 3,000 repetitions per clip, live mute/unmute/solo, seek, resource cleanup, silent exports and existing native instruments/effects/project compatibility. With 11 of those tracks muted, the stress fixture uses 12 players and one active audio file, independent of repetition count.

Build remains ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Third-party instruments, models and sound libraries are not bundled.
